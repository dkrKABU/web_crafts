ABOUT

History

Kabarak University is a private Christian university located in Nakuru, Kenya. Established in 2002, it is known for its commitment to academic excellence and holistic education. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various fields of study, including business, law, education, health sciences, and more. Kabarak University emphasizes the integration of faith and learning, fostering a supportive community that encourages personal growth and development among its students.

Location

Kabarak University is located in Nakuru, Kenya, in the Rift Valley region. It's located 23km from Nakuru. The campus is known for its serene environment and beautiful surroundings, providing an ideal setting for academic pursuits and personal development.

ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

Schools

Academic programs in Kabarak are spread across the following 7 Schools:

  1. School of Education and Social Sciences
  2. School of Business and Economics
  3. School of Medicine and Health Sciences
  4. School of Law
  5. School of Science and Technology
  6. School of Music and Media
  7. School of Pharmacy
